## 40-second version

A lot of review requests break the rules without meaning to. Google's review policy does not allow incentives for reviews, asking for a certain rating, or asking only happy customers. And since October 2024, the FTC's review rule bans incentives tied to a positive review.

The free review request checker at tryreputationflow dot com slash tools slash review-request-checker reads your text or email in your browser. It marks what to fix and what to review, explains why, links the source, and writes a short request you can send to every customer. It is not legal advice, just a fast second look before you hit send.
